# Round 3 design brief

The second batch remained eligible on all three seeds. Its mean (reward; T, W, C, M improvements in percent) was:

- R2A: 0.908; 0, +2.415, +1.837, 0.
- R2B: 0.795; 0, +2.313, +1.012, 0.
- R2C: 1.163; 0, +2.484, +4.181, 0.

Gap-responsive R2B restored the follower metric but delivered a small low-speed gain. Position taper R2C did better on both. On seed 1 its requested speed fell from 10.4 m/s at 4163 m to 7.1 at 4372 m. Yet by t=251 s the leader was close (6.58 m net gap), ego was doing 3.79 m/s, the cap was 6.52 and the following interval still contained braking at −1.38 m/s². By 257 s it was in the queue at 1.5 m/s. Keep the long taper as the incumbent. Use the final three slots to test whether approaching more gently before the merge into the queue helps, how sensitive it is to the onset of the road detector, and what happens if congestion prompts substantially earlier pacing with conditional release. Preserve every outcome, including any throughput penalty.
